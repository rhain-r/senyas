Bundled clips are unmodified original video files from clips.zip, sample 0 of
each selected label. Playback may be muted, looped or slowed by the app.
No endorsement by the creator or institution is implied.

assets/fsl_recognizer.bin contains classifier weights derived from this dataset
using hand/body landmarks, a trained neural network, and calibrated decision
thresholds. This is an adaptation; it is not an original dataset model or an
endorsement. The same CC BY 4.0 attribution applies to the derived asset.
Evaluation details and limitations are in fsl_recognizer_report.json and ml/README.md.

The pose_landmarker_lite.task and hand_landmarker.task are Google MediaPipe
models, distributed under their upstream Apache 2.0 license:
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/LICENSE
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker
